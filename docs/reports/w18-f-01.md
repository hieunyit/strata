## What

- Page `#/map` (menu "Bản đồ"): verdict switch, policy, range (1 h, 24 h,
  7 d, 30 d), filter chips; a click on a country, ASN or attack type filters
  every panel; one read for all panels, so a filter change updates them
  together (older responses are dropped).
- Panels: countries (log-scale shade, top attack type), map cells (geohash
  centers with city names), ASNs, attack types, timeline (the Overview chart).
- Live (1 h, 24 h, 7 d): the country panel from `/v1/stats/geo:watch`
  (snapshot, deltas), the other panels re-read when it changes, at most every
  10 s. 30 days: no live mode (the stream's window is at most 168 h).
- API: timeline buckets carry `error` (fail mode) like the Overview's, and
  `groupBy=attack_type`.
- Not here: base map and country/city layers drawn on it (W17-F-01, PO check
  of the borders), drill-down to events and quick rules (W19-F-01).
