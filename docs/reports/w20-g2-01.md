Plan, done when: 24 hours under 1 s and 30 days under 3 s on real data.

## What changed

The Overview counted the raw events table every time, and the map merged
per-minute distinct-count sketches. Hourly aggregates as events arrive
would count a resent batch twice, because the events table holds a
duplicate until it merges and ingest re-batches events from many agents.
Hourly sketches were measured as an alternative and rejected:

- at precision 17 they took 2.4 s at 24 h and 4.5 s at 30 days;
- at precision 12 they were fast, but off by −2.6% (events) and −6%
  (clients).

What was built is a **rollup job** (`RollupStats`, every 30 s in the
server):

- It finds the hours that received events since its last run, using
  `received_at` with a new minmax index and a 2-minute overlap.
- It recomputes them from `events FINAL`, so each event counts once, into
  ReplacingMergeTree tables (ClickHouse migration 5): per (hour, policy,
  action), rule, route, top clients and geo key.
- A recomputation replaces the hour, so it is safe to rerun or to run
  from two control planes.
- A late event, for example an agent resending after hours, is counted at
  the next run.
- The first run backfills one day at a time. Until it ends, the Overview
  reads the raw table as before.

The Overview, and the map's ranks and timeline from 6 hours up, read
these tables. Shorter map windows and the live stream keep the minute
aggregates.

**Counts of events are exact.** The response now carries
`approximate: true`, which means:

- the window is whole hours (From rounded down, To up), as the map
  already did;
- distinct clients come from a sketch (`uniqCombined64(14)`, about 1%);
- top clients are the heaviest 100 of each hour per (policy, action);
- numbers lag the events by at most the job's interval (30 s plus the
  run).

The rollup tables keep `-geo-aggregates-months`, 13 months by default.

## Tests

- `rollup_test.go` (real ClickHouse) checks the rollup against the raw
  Overview on the same window: totals, series, top rules, routes and
  clients. It also checks that:
  - a resent batch counts once;
  - a late event in an old hour and a new event are both counted at the
    next run;
  - the window rounds to whole hours;
  - geo ranks are right;
  - the TTL is set once;
  - the Overview switches to the rollup when it is ready.
- `chmigrate_clickhouse_test.go`: migration 5 and the new tables.
- `TestClickHouseStatsPerf`: the targets above, on the fixture.
- `make test-go`.
