# W20-F-01: the full Overview page

Plan, done when the page shows requests, block rate, top rules, top
routes, engine latency, faulty nodes and running rollouts, refreshes on
its own, and has a pause button.

Already on the page (W10-F-01 and later): events by verdict, the series,
top rules and routes with links to the events, top clients (for callers
allowed to see them), inspection per policy (RV-N-01), offline nodes,
the running rollout, a 30 s auto-refresh, and its pause button. On
20 million events the numbers now come from the hourly rollup
(W20-G2-01): 0.41 s over 24 hours.

## Added

- **Requests** (`totals.requests`). Every request the nodes counted in
  the window, with or without an event, summed from `policy_requests`
  (the nodes' counters, RV-N-01). The KPI shows it with the **block
  rate**: blocked events over these requests. The "blocked" KPI keeps its
  share of events. Without counters (older agents) it says so instead of
  showing 0.
- **Engine time** (`engine`). The modules already kept a histogram of
  engine time per worker in their metrics file (9 buckets: 50, 100, 200,
  500 µs, 1, 2, 5, 10 ms, more). Until now it went only to Prometheus.
  - **Agent:** its heartbeat now carries the histogram and the total,
    summed over the workers (`NodeCounters.engine_time`, `engine_ns`,
    fields 10 and 11).
  - **Control plane:** writes the increase per node and interval to
    ClickHouse `engine_time` (migration 6, 90 days), next to the
    per-policy request counts. A reload of NGINX (new
    `metrics_created_ms`) starts the sums again.
  - **Overview:** shows the window's p50, p95 and p99, each as the
    histogram bound holding it ("≤ 2 ms"; "> 10 ms" past the last
    bound), the mean, and the number of requests timed.
  - Engine time is per node, not per policy: the panel says "every
    policy and node".
- A missing statistics table on ClickHouse 25 reads "Unknown table
  expression" (code 60), not `UNKNOWN_TABLE`. The check for older
  databases (`isUnknownTable`) now matches both; the `policy_requests`
  check had the same gap.

## Tests

- `agent/internal/agent/counters_test.go`: the histogram and total are
  summed over the workers.
- `control-plane/fleet/policyrequests_test.go` checks the recorder:
  - the first heartbeat is only a starting point;
  - an interval writes its difference;
  - a heartbeat with nothing new writes nothing;
  - a decrease without a reload is skipped;
  - after a reload, all sums are new;
  - an older agent sends nothing;
  - `Forget` resets the node.
- `control-plane/ingest/enginetime_test.go`: quantiles from buckets
  (bounds, past the last one, the mean). On real ClickHouse, the window
  sum leaves out older rows, and the Overview's `engine` and
  `totals.requests` (per policy as well) are right.
- `ui/src/__tests__/Pages.test.tsx`: the requests KPI with block rate,
  the engine panel ("≤ 50 µs", "≤ 2 ms", "> 10 ms", the mean without
  "≤"), the pause toggle, and both empty states.
